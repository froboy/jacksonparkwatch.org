DNAInfo – Closing Road to Sky Through Jackson Park A “Bad Idea” Alderman Says

Posted on February 6, 2017 by Jackson Park Watch
 HYDE PARK — Fifth Ward Ald. Leslie Hairston is pushing back against plans to close major roads running through Jackson Park.

The Obama Foundation has floated plans to community leaders to close sections of Cornell Drive between 60th and 67th Street, and possibly Marquette Drive from Cornell to Lake Shore Drive….
